## What this means for the report

- **SAC's speed advantage over A2C is real** and can be stated plainly: 2.31 m/s against 1.10 m/s is clearly separated.
- **SAC's *return* advantage over A2C is not.** The means differ (1411 against 1202) but the seed spreads overlap heavily. Do not write that SAC scored higher; write that it moved faster.
- **A2C is not faster than PPO.** Their velocities overlap. A2C's advantage is that it stays upright, not that it moves quicker.
- Both SAC and A2C are clearly separated from PPO on return.

This is the seed-variance warning from Lecture 07 doing real work: two of the five comparisons we might have claimed are not supported by three seeds, and we only know that because we ran three.
